# Tables, list views, sheets and modals: defaulter report (2026-10-27)

Replaces the shorter review committed earlier today.

**Scope:** every `DataTable` (120 instances in 104 files), its list row
(`mobileRow`), slide-up sheet (`mobileRow.detail` or the auto-built one), expanded panel and
row actions; the 12 side sheets; and every modal (145 files).

**Method:** two parsers read each table's columns, filters, row actions, list row, sheet
fields/actions and expanded render, and each modal's size, inputs, form handling and layout. Rules
were then applied per row. Sections 0, 1, 6, 8 and 9 were checked by hand. Sections 2–5 and 7 are
rule-generated from the code; each row names the exact columns, fields and actions involved.
Known limits: filters or sheet fields built at runtime (hooks, props) cannot be counted statically
and are either skipped or marked "verify".

**Read §0 first.** Four of the list/sheet complaints are one bug each in `DataTable`, and fixing
them there clears most of the rows in §4 and §5 at once.
